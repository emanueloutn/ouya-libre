OuyaLibre bundles the LibVLC engine from the official VideoLAN VLC Android 3.0.13 ARMv7 release (2018), for Android 4.1 compatibility. This is a library embedded in OuyaLibre; no VLC application is required.

VideoLAN and VLC authors retain their copyrights. LibVLC and Android bindings are LGPL 2.1 or later; see LIBVLC-LGPL-2.1.txt. Original Java copyright notices are preserved. Local Java changes: removal of compile-time support annotations, replacement of support LongSparseArray with the Android API16 implementation. Native codec binaries are unchanged. libjniloader.so is replaced by the small MIT standalone JNI bootstrap in third-party/libvlc-loader/loader.c to omit the unused media-library module. Keep these notices and upstream licenses when distributing.
